Summary:

The meeting aligned on a minimal viable product for an enrollment and member-management system with the goal of delivering individual enrollment functionality by the March 1 effective date. The team reviewed the consumer enrollment flow end-to-end, including landing pages, product information and PDF support, enrollment form logic for demographics and dependents, merchant/payment flow and credit-card signature requirements, and integration requirements for vendor eligibility and reconciliation.

Participants confirmed support for individual online enrollments and CSV eligibility file feeds for list-bill/group scenarios, with member IDs auto-generated as nine-digit numbers. E-sign options were evaluated and cell-phone texting was preferred for speed and reliability while USB pads were noted for face-to-face capture. Group billing and list-bill invoicing will remain on the existing E123 system for now, and transaction and vendor eligibility outputs should be delivered as CSVs via secure transfer methods (S/FTP or equivalent). The group identified operational gaps in member eligibility files, vendor file delivery, enrollment communications, commission tracking, and broken E123 deployments, and expressed a preference for a more configurable enrollment and plan-management UI.

Decisions include a "crawl, walk, run" approach prioritizing individual enrollments first, simplified checkout/redirect-based payment capture, and maintaining current group billing processes. Open items include exact secure file formats and transfer details, broker commission handling on the new platform, and specification of portal requirements for electronic ID cards and benefit guides.

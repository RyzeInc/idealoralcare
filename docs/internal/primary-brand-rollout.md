## Shared AssureVantage cart (November 5, 2026)

`/assurevantage/program-plans` is the shared three-offering catalog. It starts with an empty cart and uses the same `assurevantage` local-storage namespace as every program and oral page. `/assurevantage/program-checkout` is the shared checkout entry; `/assurevantage/checkout` redirects there, preserving query parameters. Oral-only orders retain their selected billing cadence. Essentials and standalone Balance for Life force the whole order to monthly billing, including when restoring an old saved cart. Selecting Essentials replaces standalone BFL because it is included. Selecting a different Essentials or oral tier replaces the previous tier. The checkout API also rejects annual membership orders and Essentials/BFL combinations.

Retail checkout now uses one `UnifiedCheckout` component for Oral Care, Essentials, Balance for Life and mixed orders. All orders show Review Your Order, Payment Method, Account, Agent / Rep Selection and Confirm & Pay before authentication. The signed-in account and every required signature are needed only before proceeding to Stripe. Agreement storage failures block payment; changing the account, price, cadence or payment method invalidates prior consent.

Because Clerk requires authentication on its technical primary domain, the AssureVantage-branded public checkout and receipt are served at `https://www.getidealoh.com/assurevantage/program-checkout` and `program-success`. Visitors go to the full checkout, never a standalone sign-in page. The same inline account wizard used by legacy checkout handles account creation/sign-in in Step 3. Public catalog IDs, cadence, payment method and rep attribution cross domains in a validated, single-use URL selection; no profile, password, payment credentials or supplied prices are transferred. Current visible/allowed catalog products reconstruct the cart. Return links carry shopping edits back to AssureVantage, including an emptied cart. Existing Ideal marketing and its original checkout destinations remain unchanged.
